MISC & USEFUL

Grocery, pharmacy, wine & liquor, and other everyday errands near the house.

Groceries & Convenience
  • Stop n' Shop — Large grocery store. No alcohol. Bagged ice available.
  • By the Shore Convenience — Very small convenience store, walking distance to the house. Bagged ice available.
  • Shorecrest Store — Small convenience store, walking distance. Bagged ice available.
Pharmacy
  • CVS — Pharmacy open 9 AM–1:30 PM & 2–9 PM.
Wine & Liquor
  • Ocean State Liquors — Pretty large liquor store. Bagged ice available.
  • The Wine Store — Smaller store, not a great wine selection. Bagged ice available.
  • Dick's World of Wines — Better wine selection. Bagged ice available.
Bakery
  • Vesta Bakery — Your best bet for cakes or other special event orders.
Emergency & Medical
  • Westerly Hospital — Closest option for both emergency and urgent care.
Gas Station
  • Mobil — Closest gas station.
ATM & Banking
  • Closest ATM is at the Pleasant View Inn.
  • Lots of banks are located on Rt. 1.
Post Office & Shipping
  • US Post Office
  • UPS — inside the Staples.
Hardware
  • McQuade's Ace Hardware
Dry Cleaning
  • Pier Cleaners
