EXPLORE

South County is full of amazing towns brimming with history, great food, and fun kiddo activities.

Welcome to Westerly, Rhode Island! Whether you're here for a relaxing getaway, a family vacation, or a bit of adventure, you've chosen the perfect destination. Nestled along the scenic coastline, Westerly offers a unique mix of small-town charm, beautiful beaches, and vibrant culture. From historic landmarks to local eateries, and from outdoor activities to live entertainment, there's something here for everyone.

Westerly is known for its stunning coastline, with miles of sandy beaches perfect for sunbathing, swimming, or enjoying a peaceful walk. Misquamicut State Beach is one of the most popular spots, offering family-friendly amenities, beautiful ocean views, and seasonal events like outdoor concerts and drive-in movies.

Hiking Trails

Trail lengths and difficulty per AllTrails / park managers. Distance is approximate drive time from the house.

Trail Distance Length Difficulty Notes
Dr. John Champlin Glacier Park
Winnapaug		 ~5 min 2.3 mi loop Easy Closest trail to the house. Hilly kettle & kame terrain with a view over Winnapaug Pond from Charlies Overlook.
Wahaneeta Preserve
Westerly		 ~8 min ~3.8 mi of trails Easy–moderate Wooded wetlands with a pond and stream, next to the Little League complex.
Westerly Town Forest ~10 min 2.2 mi loop Easy Well-marked multi-loop trails along the Pawcatuck River.
Napatree Point Conservation Area
Watch Hill		 ~10 min 3.2 mi loop Easy, flat Oceanside walk with great views. Best at low tide — skip if waves are hitting the rocks.
Ninigret Park Bike Path
Charlestown		 ~10 min 2.2 mi loop, paved Easy Flat paved loop around the old airfield — good for strollers and bikes too.
Ninigret National Wildlife Refuge
Charlestown		 ~12 min 4+ mi of trails Easy Salt marsh and pond views. Great birding.
Grills Preserve
Bradford		 ~15 min 2.7–4.4 mi (route-dependent) Easy Shaded forest, ponds, marsh, and a historic cemetery. Bring bug spray.
Burlingame State Park
Charlestown		 ~15 min Up to 8 mi (shorter loops available) Easy–moderate Oak & birch forest around Watchaug Pond. Some rocky/rooty sections.
Long Pond Woods
Hopkinton		 ~20–25 min 2.7–4.4 mi Hard Reputed to be the most challenging (and one of the most beautiful) hikes in RI — steep, rocky terrain with mountain laurel. No dogs or trail running.
